# Entry draft — Hurry Up and Wait

**Title:** Hurry Up and Wait

**Short description:** One racer, one courier, one departure. An original 21-second Blender miniature about the difference between moving fast and arriving sooner.

**Description:**
A red racer tears down its lane and reaches the ferry barrier first. While it fidgets and spins its wheels, a calm cyan courier rolls up just in time. They board together and cross at exactly the same speed.

The Speed theme drives the timing and the joke. The camera moves closer while red waits, then opens back out as the shared departure resolves the race.

**Procedural workflow:**
The scene was created in Blender with a Codex-assisted Python/bpy script. The script constructs the vehicles, dock, barrier, ferry, materials, lighting, typography and camera, then creates the animation from deterministic paths and keyframes. Tire rotation tracks travel during approach and boarding; both vehicles then remain fixed relative to the ferry. All motion is choreographed rather than physically simulated. The stylized water does not claim fluid simulation. Original synthetic Foley was generated from simple oscillators in a separate Python script.

**AI and asset disclosure:**
Codex assisted concept comparison, source generation, debugging and QA. No downloaded models, textures, music or sound samples were used. Geometry, materials and Foley are original to this entry. Typography uses Blender's bundled Bfont. Blender and FFmpeg performed the local render and encoding.
